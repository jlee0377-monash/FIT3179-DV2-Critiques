Farmers’ Markets: Where They Gather, When They Happen

An in-depth visual exploration blending national AFMA directory patterns with Brisbane community event dynamics.

This visualisation integrates the Australian Farmers’ Markets Association (AFMA) national directory with Brisbane City Council event listings[cite: 15]. By combining these two distinct data sources, this narrative offers complementary insights into regional agricultural distribution, consumer access, and urban event scheduling[cite: 15].

Synthesis & Key Takeaways

The supplied AFMA directory contains more listings for New South Wales and Victoria than for other represented states. Brisbane event records show scheduled events across dates, venues and event categories. These datasets describe listings and schedules; they do not measure attendance, sales, stall diversity or the causes of geographic differences.

Data Sources: AFMA Market Directory (AFMA_markets.csv) and Brisbane City Council Event Listings (brisbane-markets.csv)[cite: 15].
